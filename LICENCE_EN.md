#### 6. Contributor Compensation

- **Compensation Principle**: Revenue generated by Open Saga stories will be distributed among contributors based on the size of their contribution as measured by the number of words added.

- **Visual Contribution Equivalence**:
  - Simple illustration (sketch, diagram): equivalent to **2,000 words**.
  - Detailed illustration (drawing, digital painting): equivalent to **5,000 words**.
  - Complex illustration (comic page, complete artwork): equivalent to **10,000 words**.
  - Categorization is determined by the principal author in consultation with active contributors.

- **Principal Author's Share**:
  - The principal author retains **at least 50% of the revenue** generated by each work, regardless of their specific contributions.
  - If the principal author makes significant contributions to a work, their share may increase proportionally to the importance and size of their contributions.
- **Revenue Distribution**:
  - The remaining 50% (or less, if the principal author increases their share) is distributed among other contributors.
  - The distribution is based on the total number of words added by each co-author in proportion to the total number of words added by all co-authors. For example, if a co-author contributes 1,000 words to a story where all co-authors have contributed a total of 10,000 words, that co-author will receive 10% of the remaining 50% revenue.
  - Visual contributions are converted to word equivalents according to the scale above.
- **Contribution Validation**: Co-authors will be compensated based on the size of their contribution only if it is accepted by at least 65% of **active contributors** who have interacted on the project. Inactive contributors are not counted in this vote. The principal author reserves the right to decide in case of a deadlock or if the number of active contributors is fewer than three.
